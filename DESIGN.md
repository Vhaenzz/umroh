# Risalah Madina Tour visual direction

## Identity

Risalah Madina Tour is a human-led Umroh and Haji travel service. The interface should feel like a careful conversation with a real team: calm, clear about money and schedules, grounded in documentary photography, and easy for families to use on a phone.

## Audience

Indonesian prospective pilgrims and their families who need to compare dates, hotels, room options, facilities, payment plans, and registration steps before contacting the team.

## Visual language

- Deep teal is the anchor for trust, navigation, legal information, and important headings.
- Olive is the single active accent. It marks the next action or a real status, never decoration on every component.
- White and warm grey surfaces keep long package and financing information readable.
- Real journey photography is the identity motif. Use it to show places, people, and the service itself instead of generic illustration.
- Thin rules, documentary image crops, and editorial two-column compositions provide structure without adding visual noise.

## Typography

Use Plus Jakarta Sans throughout. It was chosen for legibility across Indonesian body copy, price tables, and mobile forms. Weight and size create hierarchy; wide uppercase tracking is reserved for short metadata labels.

## Composition and behavior

The homepage leads with one clear action: compare a real package. Supporting sections alternate between package data, destinations, service proof, and the practical steps a pilgrim needs. On mobile, horizontal rails are intentional for browsing a small set of package or destination cards; long-form content reflows into one readable column and tables remain explicitly scrollable.

## Liveliness dials

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2

- ENERGY 2: a confident olive action against deep teal, with real photography doing most of the emotional work.
- RHYTHM 2: a few composition changes between catalogue, editorial image, and practical information sections, without novelty for its own sake.
- MOTION 2: short state transitions and the existing ambient hero video support orientation; no endless decorative pulses or stacked entrance animations.

## Guardrails

- No generic blue-purple gradients, glow fields, background grids, or fake metrics.
- No invented testimonials, people, customer counts, hotel claims, or certification claims.
- Every navigation link and CTA must lead to a real page, a real package, WhatsApp, mail, or an in-page section.
- Every color, radius, shadow, and image treatment must support hierarchy, readability, or recognition of the travel service.
