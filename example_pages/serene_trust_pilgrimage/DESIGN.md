## Brand & Style

This design system embodies *sakinah* (tranquility), *amanah* (uncompromising trust and integrity), and *bersahaja* (humble elegance). Designed specifically for a premium Haji and Umroh pilgrimage service, the visual narrative distances itself from common tropes—avoiding loud, saturated neon greens, reflective yellow foil golds, or cliché geometric arabesques.

Instead, the aesthetic draws inspiration from quiet sanctuary courtyards, tactile parchment manuscripts, and deep aged architectural stone. The experience must feel profoundly calming, reverent, and reassuring, alleviating the logistical anxiety often associated with sacred journeys.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Quiet Editorial Elegance:** Clean layouts supported by generous negative space, sophisticated serif typography, and balanced proportional grids.
- **Organic Depth & Subtle Tactility:** Warm parchment backgrounds combined with whisper-soft hairline borders and muted metallic accents reminiscent of hand-brushed brass.
- **Reverence & Restraint:** Visual elements prioritize clarity, contemplation, and dignified guidance rather than commercial aggressiveness.

## Colors

The palette is tuned to evoke serenity, grounded authority, and timeless spiritual peace. Contrast is preserved with dignity, using near-black ink on parchment-tinted surfaces.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#0F3838`, deep serene teal):** Represents depth, dignity, and calm authority. Serves as the primary anchor for navigation bars, primary actions, and prominent framing headers.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`, brushed warm brass):** A restrained, muted gold that accents rather than dominates. Used selectively for pilgrimage milestones, sacred indicators, key verification badges, and refined hairline rules.
- **Tertiary / Success (`#1E7B58`, calm emerald):** A grounded, herbal forest-tinted green indicating verified paperwork, confirmed visa status, and successful bookings without aggressive saturation.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF9F5`, warm parchment off-white):** Evokes unbleached linen and classical manuscripts, preventing the eye fatigue caused by clinical sterile whites.
- **Neutral Surface Elevated (`#F4EFEA`, tinted limestone):** Provides soft structural separation for cards and containers without heavy lines.
- **Text & Ink (`#1A2024`, deep slate charcoal):** Softens the visual harshness of pure black while maintaining WCAG AAA compliant readability.
- **Warning (`#B7791F`, amber ochre):** Signals actionable steps, such as document expirations and pending payments.
- **Critical (`#991B1B`, quiet terracotta crimson):** Indicates urgent requirements or cancellations with solemnity rather than alarm.

## Typography

The typographical pairing creates a deliberate dialogue between classical sacred literature and modern, utilitarian clarity.

### Pairing Strategy
- **Playfair Display:** Chosen for its dignified, transitional serifs, high stroke contrast, and poetic restraint. It anchors emotional and contemplative touchpoints—pilgrimage package names, contemplative Quranic/Hadith excerpts, section chapters, and milestone steps.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Provides clear, contemporary Indonesian geometric legibility. Used across long-form logistical guidance, ritual schedules, itinerary timelines, pricing, and form fields. It ensures zero reading strain for elderly pilgrims and family sponsors.

### Typographic Principles
- Keep headline line lengths restrained (maximum 45–60 characters) to preserve an editorial, tranquil cadence.
- Itinerary dates and numerical pricing must always render in tabular or clean sans-serif figures for exact clarity.
- Avoid all-caps styling in Playfair Display; reserve uppercase treatment exclusively for `label-sm` category headers set in letterspaced Plus Jakarta Sans.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy relies on an intentional rhythm of breathing room and proportional balance. Content is presented in an unhurried, step-by-step structure to avoid cognitive overload during complex booking and verification flows.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with a centered maximum content container of 1280px. Outer margins sit at `3rem` (48px) with internal column gutters at `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with flexible side margins of `2rem` (32px) and gutters of `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout with edge margins of `1.25rem` (20px) and gutters of `1rem` (16px).

### Section Rhythms
- Major content transitions (such as shifting from hotel accommodations in Makkah/Madinah to visa documentation) require generous vertical rhythm, utilizing `space-xl` or double `space-xl` (4rem to 5rem) of whitespace.
- Form controls, visa checklists, and daily prayer itinerary items remain neatly clustered with standard `space-md` gaps to maintain grouping clarity.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid plastic or artificial modern visuals, elevation relies on **tonal stepping** and **warm ambient illumination** rather than crisp drop shadows.

### Elevation Architecture
1. **Base Tier (Ground):** The parchment backdrop (`#FBF9F5`).
2. **Container Tier (Level 1):** Tinted limestone surfaces (`#F4EFEA`) defined by an ultra-delicate border: `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)` (a 20% opacity warm brass stroke).
3. **Elevated Cards (Level 2):** Pure soft white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) featuring an ambient shadow with a warm undertone: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 56, 56, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`.
4. **Modals & Sticky Drawers (Level 3):** Floated elements cast a deep, diffused, calming teal-tinted shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 56, 56, 0.14)`.

Frosted blur effects are restricted to sticky headers: 12px blur with an 85% alpha primary teal or parchment surface to preserve legibility without feeling synthetic.

## Shapes

The design system uses **Soft** geometry (`0.25rem` / 4px base, `0.5rem` / 8px for containers, and `0.75rem` / 12px for primary feature cards).

### Shape Philosophy
- **Architectural Poise:** Overly rounded or pill-shaped designs dilute the gravitas and structural solemnity of sacred heritage architecture. Sharp-to-soft corners echo hand-trimmed manuscript pages, stone masonry, and framed courtyard portals.
- **Pills Reserved for Singular Tags:** Only tiny status chips (e.g., "Sisa 4 Kursi", "Pembimbing Bersertifikasi") may use full pill radii to maintain immediate distinction from actionable interactive buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid deep teal (`#0F3838`) background with light warm parchment text (`#FBF9F5`). Border radius of 6px (`rounded-soft`). Hover transitions into a luminous petroleum shade (`#164E4D`) with an inner ambient glow.
- **Secondary / Sacred Action:** Transparent background bounded by a `1.5px` brushed brass border (`#C5A059`) with `#0F3838` text. Hover adds a warm wash of `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.1)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Pure `#0F3838` text with an underlined `1px` brass accent that animates outward on interaction.

### Cards (Package & Itinerary)
- Wrapped in Level 2 elevation with a discrete 1px border (`rgba(22, 78, 77, 0.08)`).
- Card headers utilize Playfair Display with brass badges displaying flight tiers (e.g., "Direct Saudia / Garuda Indonesia") and hotel distances (e.g., "Pelataran Masjidil Haram - 50m").
- Footers feature prominent, unhurried cost summaries with transparent inclusions and payment milestone structures.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Indicators:** Subtle tinted background fills (10% opacity of status hex) paired with solid matching status text (`#1E7B58` for verified/quad, `#B7791F` for waiting list/double room options).
- Padding is compact (`space-xs` vertical, `space-sm` horizontal) using `label-md`.

### Input Fields
- Understated warm parchment fills (`#F4EFEA`) with a bottom-weighted `1.5px` border in `#D4B068` (40% opacity).
- Focused states smoothly transition to a deep teal outline (`#0F3838`) with a subtle 2px soft brass ambient ring (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`).
- Helper copy appears in neutral slate body-sm with clear requirements for passport validation, mahram details, and health documentation.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square checkboxes with soft 3px radii. Active state fills with deep serene teal (`#0F3838`) featuring an off-white checkmark.
- Radio buttons feature a concentric brushed brass circle ringed inside deep teal borders.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Ritual Step Progress Bar (Manasik & Travel Tracker):** An elegant, horizontal step flow alternating between completed, current, and upcoming stages (e.g., Ihram, Tawaf, Sa'i, Tahallul, Madinah Ziyarah). Active stages are signaled by an illuminated brass beacon with a soft teal pulse.
- **Hotel Distance Indicator:** A micro-gauge contrasting walking proximity to the Haram and Nabawi courtyards, presented with dignified clarity rather than aggressive sales tactics.
- **Muthawif / Guide Credential Card:** Dedicated profile containers honoring the scholar/guide, highlighting their Islamic university credentials, years of guidance, and language capabilities.
